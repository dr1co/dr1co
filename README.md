## 🚌 My journey in the Binary World

<details>
<summary>Read the story</summary>

My first contact with programming was in 2019 with C++, during my first semester of a Bachelor's in Chemical Engineering at UFPR (Federal University of Paraná). In my opinion, it was one of the most enjoyable subjects that semester, but I kept going with Chemical Engineering. I only really dove headfirst into programming in 2022, three years later.

That year, I left Chemical Engineering to pursue a career in software. I started with an online bootcamp of over 1,200 hours, where I learned the fundamentals of programming and web development with HTML, CSS and JS, while building my first web applications with React. I didn't want to stop there, so in 2023 I started a second Bachelor's degree, in Software Engineering, at UEPG (State University of Ponta Grossa).

My knowledge of and passion for software development have grown ever since. I've taken part in hackathons, projects and coding challenges that brought me closer to what I work with today: automation and DevOps. Nowadays, I love exploring the whole development flow of a project, from the first file edit to the final steps of delivering the solution.
